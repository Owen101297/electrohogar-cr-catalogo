## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a direct, authoritative, and deeply practical direct-selling retail experience tailored for the Costa Rican home goods market. It blends the structural reliability of trusted local institutions with the friction-free immediacy required for door-to-door sales consultants presenting catalogs on mobile devices and tablets under direct sunlight.

The design movement is **Corporate / Modern** anchored in high-clarity retail functionality. It balances institutional safety with promotional urgency:
- **Trust & Authority:** Grounded in deep sapphire blue to evoke commercial longevity, formal guarantees, and installment credit credibility.
- **Retail Vigor:** Dynamic promotional gold accents signal hot offers, discounts, and clear financing tags without visual clutter.
- **Conversational Commerce:** Integrated high-contrast messaging cues prioritize rapid closing loops via WhatsApp.
- **Field Ergonomics:** High touch targets, strong contrast ratios, and scannable visual anchors optimized for quick street-level consultations.

## Colors

The color architecture communicates financial stability, retail excitement, and rapid conversion pathways:

- **Primary (`#0F4C81`)**: Deep sapphire blue representing the institutional anchor. Applied to primary brand headers, navigation bars, top-level status bars, active states, and core category tags.
- **Secondary (`#EAB308`)**: Vibrant retail gold. Reserved exclusively for high-impact promotional markers: discount percentages, flash offers, installment badges (*"Cuotas desde"*), and attention-grabbing chips.
- **Tertiary (`#25D366`)**: Direct sales engine green. Strictly reserved for real-time contact touchpoints, direct checkout inquiries, quote requests, and floating consultation triggers.
- **Neutral (`#212B36`)**: High-legibility slate charcoal for primary product names, specifications, and colón currency values. Never use pure `#000000` for body typography to maintain visual softness against white backdrops.
- **Semantic Accents**:
  - `promo-amber` (`#F59E0B`): Used for secondary retail tags, ratings, and warranty banners.
  - `retail-red` (`#DF2421`): Applied strictly to urgency timers, clearance tags (*"Liquidación"*), and error/out-of-stock validation.
  - `surface-canvas` (`#F4F6F8`): Crisp background layer that isolates product photography.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the structured geometric impact of **Montserrat** with the systematic, ultra-legible clarity of **Inter**:

- **Headlines & Financial Impact (Montserrat)**: Delivers punchy retail presence. High-weight Montserrat is deployed specifically for product nomenclature, promotional hero statements, and price figures in colones (`₡`). Large numbers must stand out immediately when tilting the phone toward a customer.
- **Narrative & Specifications (Inter)**: Handles descriptive details, capacity metrics, technical specifications, and warranty disclaimers with zero ambiguity.
- **Currency Presentation**: The Costa Rican Colón symbol (`₡`) shares the identical weight and size of the adjoining integer. It is never superscripted or subdued. Strikethrough cash prices are rendered in medium gray alongside bright primary credit rates.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout system is tailored primarily for handheld mobile screens (360px–430px wide) used by sales advisors, scaling cleanly to tablets (768px–1024px) for side-by-side presentation.

- **Grid Model**: A fluid 4-column system on mobile devices, transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablets and 12 columns on desktop admin views.
- **Touch-First Tap Targets**: Elements intended for advisor or customer interaction strictly maintain a minimum vertical clearance of 48px to prevent mis-taps during in-person presentations.
- **Catalog Density**: Product listing views employ a compact two-column card layout on mobile (`gutter-mobile: 0.75rem`), shifting to full-width horizontal presentation cards during deep-dive detail views.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses crisp ambient separation rather than heavy skeuomorphic shading, guaranteeing clarity in high-glare environments:

- **Surface Tiers**: Base canvas rests at `#F4F6F8`. White product cards (`#FFFFFF`) sit on top with a hairline border (`1px solid #DFE3E8`) paired with an ultra-soft ambient shadow (`0 2px 8px rgba(15, 76, 129, 0.06)`).
- **Interactive Depth**: Elevated elements like the floating inquiry button and promotional sheets utilize a focused direct drop shadow (`0 8px 24px rgba(15, 76, 129, 0.16)`).
- **Modal Sheets**: Bottom drawer sheets (used for financing calculators and technical comparisons) introduce an 80% opacity neutral backdrop overlay (`#212B36CC`) with an immediate physical presence.

## Shapes

The roundedness factor of `2` provides a balanced, approachable profile (8px default radius) that balances modern software aesthetics with durable hardware retail styling.

- **Standard Elements (0.5rem / 8px)**: Applied to input controls, secondary buttons, product cards, and installment calculator modules.
- **Promotional Badges (0.25rem / 4px)**: Applied to sale percentage stickers and specification chips for a sharper, crisp paper-tag feel.
- **Action Triggers & Floats (Full Pill / 9999px)**: Reserved for floating action buttons (WhatsApp quick-quote triggers) and main conversion pills to create clear affordance contrast against square product cards.

## Components

### Product Cards
- **Structure**: Vertical arrangement featuring an isolated white photography canvas, dynamic upper-left tag row, upper-right guarantee pill, product title in Montserrat (`headline-sm`), cash price, and emphasized monthly installment calculation.
- **Promotional Badges**: Background in `secondary_color_hex` (`#EAB308`) or `retail-red` (`#DF2421`) with crisp white or dark neutral text, showcasing `-30% OFF` or `PREVENTA`.

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Direct WhatsApp Action**: Solid `#25D366` surface with high-contrast `#FFFFFF` text and accompanying icon. Full-width on mobile with an 8px border radius.
- **Primary Retail Action**: Solid `#0F4C81` surface with white text for technical sheet downloads or cart reservations.
- **Financing Toggle**: Segmented pill controls allowing sales agents to alternate between "Contado" (Cash) and "Cuotas Quincenales / Mensuales" (Bi-weekly / Monthly installments).

### Catalog Inputs & Filtration
- **Field Search**: High-contrast white input field with an inner `#0F4C81` search icon, 48px height, and clear filter chips beneath for major home categories: *Refrigeración*, *Lavado*, *Cocina*, *Audio/Video*.
- **Quick Filters**: Horizontal scrolling chip container with active chips highlighted in deep sapphire with white typography.

### Floating Action Trigger
- **Field Selling Floating Button**: Fixed pill at the bottom-right corner (or pinned bottom bar on mobile screens) featuring the WhatsApp icon and bold label: *"Cotizar por WhatsApp"*. Elevated with depth tier to remain visibly dominant over catalog content.
